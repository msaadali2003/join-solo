What is Solo?

Solo is a trust-first marketplace built to connect customers with skilled local professionals. With clear profiles, secure bookings and honest reviews, every experience is a reliable one.

Who is Solo for?

Built as a trust-based community for everyone, Solo bridges the gap in local hiring. We help customers choose services with total confidence, and help professionals build the visibility, reputation and long-term client relationships they need to thrive.

What services will be available?

Solo is designed for a wide range of local and professional services, including education, creative arts, beauty, health and care, domestic help, and maintenance and repair.

How does Solo help customers choose?

Customers can review profiles, ratings, service details, past work and clear expectations before booking.

Can professionals join before launch?

Yes. Professionals can register interest now and be part of the early Solo community.

Will Solo be on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Solo is planned for both iOS and Android.
